# Travel Approval

Employees must receive manager approval before arranging business travel.

Travel requests should explain the business purpose, destination, expected dates, and estimated expenses.

# Travel Reservations

Employees should follow company travel procedures when booking transportation and accommodations.

# Travel Expenses

Approved business travel expenses may be submitted through the company's expense system.

Employees should retain receipts and provide the business purpose for submitted expenses.

# Travel Assistance

Employees with questions about travel approval should contact their manager. Questions about reimbursement should be directed to Finance.
