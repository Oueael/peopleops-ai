# First Day Responsibilities

New employees should review their welcome materials and confirm that they can access the company's required systems.

Employees should contact their manager if they have not received their initial schedule or onboarding instructions.

# Required Accounts

New employees may need access to email, collaboration tools, internal documentation, and role-specific applications.

Access requests should be submitted through the appropriate IT process. Managers are responsible for identifying the systems required for each employee's role.

# Initial Training

New employees should complete assigned orientation and security training.

Training assignments may vary depending on the employee's department and responsibilities.

# Manager Responsibilities

Managers should introduce new employees to their team and explain the employee's initial responsibilities.

Managers should identify a colleague who can help answer routine questions during the employee's first weeks.

# First Week Check-In

Managers should schedule an initial check-in during the employee's first week.

The discussion should cover access to required tools, initial responsibilities, and any obstacles affecting the employee's ability to begin work.

# Thirty-Day Check-In

Managers should review the employee's progress during the first month.

The conversation may cover training progress, role expectations, and questions about team processes.

# Onboarding Assistance

Employees who need help with onboarding should first contact their manager.

Questions involving account access or technical problems should be directed to IT support.

Questions about benefits, employment policies, or workplace procedures should be directed to People Operations.

# Onboarding Records

Managers should confirm that required onboarding activities have been completed.

Employees should follow instructions provided by their department for submitting required onboarding documentation.

# Remote Employee Onboarding

Remote employees receive onboarding instructions electronically.

Managers should confirm that remote employees can access required systems and know how to contact their team.

# Onboarding Feedback

Employees may provide feedback about their onboarding experience to People Operations.

Feedback can identify missing documentation, access problems, or areas where instructions could be clearer.
