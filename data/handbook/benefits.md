# Benefits Enrollment

New employees have 30 days from their
employment start date to enroll in benefits.

Available benefits include medical insurance,
dental insurance, vision coverage, and
retirement savings plans.

# Benefits Questions

Employees who need assistance with benefits
enrollment should contact the People Operations
team.

The People Operations team can provide
information about enrollment deadlines and
available plans.
