# Eligible Business Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing approved work activities.

Examples include approved business travel, required work supplies, and authorized business meals.

# Expense Submission

Employees must submit expense reports through the company's expense system.

Each submission should include the transaction date, amount, business purpose, and supporting receipt when available.

# Expense Review

Managers review submitted expenses for business purpose and policy compliance. Additional review may be required for unusual or high-value expenses.

# Reimbursement Questions

Employees with questions about reimbursement status should contact the Finance department.
