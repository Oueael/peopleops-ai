# Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid
time off annually.

PTO accrues monthly throughout the calendar
year. Employees may request time off through
the company's HR portal.

# PTO Approval

Employees should submit PTO requests at least
two weeks before their intended leave.

Managers are responsible for reviewing requests
within three business days.

Approval depends on staffing requirements
and business operations.
