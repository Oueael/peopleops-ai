# Required Security Training

Employees must complete security awareness training assigned by the company.

Training may cover phishing, password protection, safe handling of company information, and reporting suspicious activity.

# Phishing Reports

Employees who receive a suspicious email should report it using the company's designated security reporting process.

Employees should avoid opening unexpected attachments or entering credentials into unfamiliar websites.

# Training Assistance

Employees who cannot access assigned training should contact IT support.
