# Equipment Requests

Employees who need a laptop, monitor, keyboard, or other standard work equipment should submit a request through the IT support portal.

# Equipment Approval

Equipment requests are reviewed by the IT department. Additional approval may be required for specialized or nonstandard equipment.

# Damaged Equipment

Employees should report damaged or malfunctioning company equipment to IT as soon as possible. Employees should not attempt unauthorized hardware repairs.
