# Company Holidays

The company publishes its observed holiday schedule before the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees should consult the official company holiday calendar for specific dates.

# Holiday Scheduling

Some departments may require staffing coverage during observed holidays. Managers are responsible for coordinating coverage with their teams.

# Holiday Questions

Employees who have questions about holiday scheduling should contact People Operations.
