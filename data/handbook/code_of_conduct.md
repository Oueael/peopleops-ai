# Professional Conduct

Employees are expected to treat coworkers with professionalism and respect.

The company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or retaliation in the workplace.

# Reporting Concerns

Employees who experience or witness inappropriate workplace conduct may report their concerns to People Operations.

Employees may also use the company's designated reporting channels.

# Confidentiality

Reports involving workplace conduct should be handled carefully. Employees should avoid sharing sensitive details with people who are not involved in handling the concern.

# Immediate Assistance

Employees who believe they are in immediate danger should contact appropriate emergency services.
