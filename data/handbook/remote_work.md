# Remote Work Eligibility

Employees may request remote work arrangements through their manager. Approval depends on job responsibilities, team needs, and operational requirements.

# Remote Work Expectations

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during their agreed working hours and attend scheduled team meetings.

Employees must protect company information when working outside the office. Confidential documents should not be left visible to unauthorized individuals.

# Remote Work Equipment

Employees should contact IT for assistance with company-issued equipment used for remote work.
